1"""FlexOlmo architecture adapter. 

2 

3AllenAI's FlexOlmo (``FlexOlmoForCausalLM``, NeurIPS 2025): federated MoE 

4built by merging independently trained OLMo-2 experts, enabling 

5inference-time data opt-out by expert selection. Structurally it is the 

6exact union of the two OLMo variants already supported: OLMo-2's post-norm 

7blocks and full-width q/k norms, with OLMoE's batched-parameter sparse MoE 

8(gate_up_proj/down_proj as 3D tensors behind a top-k router) in place of 

9the dense MLP. The router is a raw-parameter module (not nn.Linear), so it 

10is wrapped for hooks as a plain delegated component.
